Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time and the change applies from your next billing date.

The Team plan includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card is needed to start.

Where is my data stored?

In EU data centres by default. Enterprise customers can choose a specific region.

What happens if I cancel?

You can download everything for 30 days. After that, your files are permanently deleted.
